# List of species known to `birdsize`

A table of the AOU (Pardieck et al 2019), genus, and species of the 443
species with species-specific data built in to `birdsize`.

## Usage

``` r
known_species
```

## Format

A data frame with 443 rows and 6 variables:

- AOU:

  AOU used in Paradieck et al. (2019)

- genus:

  genus, from Paradieck et al. (2019)

- species:

  species, from Paradieck et al. (2019)

## References

- Pardieck, K.L., Ziolkowski Jr., D.J., Lutmerding, M., Aponte, V., and
  Hudson, M-A.R., 2019, North American Breeding Bird Survey Dataset
  1966 - 2018 (ver. 2018.0): U.S. Geological Survey, Patuxent Wildlife
  Research Center, https://doi.org/10.5066/P9HE8XYJ.
